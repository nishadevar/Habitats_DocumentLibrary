What kind of access are you giving?

Choose Data Transfer to let the recipient view a live copy, or Data Ownership to hand over edit rights as well. This determines which items you can pick next.

Data Transfer
Recipient gets a live link with view-only access. Only Approved items are eligible. Best for client transparency and collaboration.
Data Ownership
Recipient gets full edit rights and becomes responsible for the item. Draft or Approved items are eligible. Needs admin approval.
